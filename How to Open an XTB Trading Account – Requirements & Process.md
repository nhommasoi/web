## **Steps to Open an XTB Account**

Opening an account with XTB involves a few key steps, typically completed online:

### **1. Registration**

* **[Visit the XTB Website](https://link-pso.xtb.com/pso/z78D7):** Go to the official XTB website.
* **Click "Create Account" or "Register":** You'll find this button prominently displayed on the homepage.
* **Fill in the Online Form:** Provide your email address, choose your country of residence, and create a secure password. Accept the terms and conditions.

### **2. Providing Personal Details**

* **Complete the Registration Form:** Enter your personal information, including your title, full name, place and date of birth, nationality, and national identification number.
* **Confirm Non-US National Status:** Indicate that you are not a US national and that you are the rightful owner of the funds you will deposit.

### **3. Client Suitability Test**

* **Assess Your Trading Knowledge and Experience:** XTB needs to ensure you understand the risks involved in trading leveraged instruments.
* **Answer Questions About Your Trading History:** You'll be asked about your experience with shares, bonds, futures, options, and other financial instruments.
* **Provide Information About Your Trading Volume and Leverage:** Specify your average annual trading volume, the leverage ratios you typically use, and the average position size you intend to trade at XTB.
* **Financial Qualification:** Provide information about your level of education or qualifications in the financial markets.

### **4. KYC Verification**

* **Verify Your Identity and Address:** XTB is required to comply with Know Your Customer (KYC) regulations.
* **Upload Identity Documents:** Provide a copy of your passport, national ID card (front and back), or driving license (front and back). Ensure the document is valid and clearly shows your photo and personal details.
* **Upload Proof of Address:** Provide a recent utility bill (gas, electricity, water), bank statement, or tax statement. The document must be dated within the last three months and show your full name and address.
* **Upload Documents:** You can upload photos, scans, or screenshots of your documents. Ensure all information is readable. File sizes should not exceed 12 MB.

### **5. Account Activation**

* **Wait for Document Verification:** XTB will review your documents to verify your identity and address. This typically takes a few minutes to a few hours.
* **Account Activation:** Once your documents are approved, your account will be activated, and you will receive a notification.

### **6. Funding Your Account**

* **Deposit Funds:** Once your account is activated, you can deposit funds using various methods, including bank transfers, credit/debit cards, and electronic wallets.
* **Start Trading:** After funding your account, you can start trading the wide range of instruments available on the XTB platform.
